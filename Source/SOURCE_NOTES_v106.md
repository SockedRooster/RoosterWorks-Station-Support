# v1.0.6 battery fit

After the documented v1.0.3 build, apply_v104.py and apply_v105.py, run `python Source/apply_v106.py`. Alternatively supply `--baseline-zip <v1.0.5-player.zip>`, which also verifies the entire game-data delta. A SHA256 guard rejects mismatched input models; to reapply, use the untouched v1.0.5 baseline ZIP.

Source/BatteryMaps maps each merged body-mesh vertex to a Battery_Face assembly. These maps were generated by tracking original FBX geometry IDs through the converter. The generated Hex body vertices, normals, UVs, triangles and material IDs matched the released v1.0.5 model data exactly before the maps were accepted. The converter now offers optional metadata_path and keeps per-vertex source IDs to support future audits. The saved maps make the final fix standard-library-only and reproducible.

For each face, the builder measures the minimum and maximum battery projection along the face normal and the inner armor plane. It compresses the battery assembly's radial extent about its minimum projection to end 5 mm inside the armor. Tangential position and Y are unchanged. Normals receive the affine inverse-transpose and are renormalized. This preserves the inner battery plane instead of translating the whole battery toward the tanks.

Only vertex/normal byte spans identified by the source maps are edited. White/grey panels have identical geometry and remain unchanged, as do materials, UVs, triangle indices and all colliders. The direct binary update avoids any unrelated FBX re-export differences. Apply this as the final release model step; plain FBX conversion alone does not include the final clearance fix.

Medium battery assembly depth: 77.07 -> 62.00 mm. Long: 77.07 -> 22.00 mm. Height and tangential width unchanged. Initial resource values/capacities, price and dry mass unchanged.
